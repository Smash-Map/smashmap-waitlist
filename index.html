What we do with your data
  • Signing up creates your SmashMap account, with your email, password (hashed, unreadable even to us) and crew code. Nothing else.
  • Why: to tell you when we launch and let you in with your crew. Legal basis: your consent.
  • How long: until launch, then 3 months at most if you never use the app.
  • Nobody else has access. No resale, no ads, no advertising trackers on this page.
  • To be removed from the list or see your data: contact@smashmap.app. You can also contact the CNIL, the French data protection authority.
